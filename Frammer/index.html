What is multi-head attention and why does it help?

Multi-head attention runs several attention functions in parallel, each learning a different representation subspace [S1]. Their outputs are concatenated and projected, letting the model jointly attend to information from different positions [S2].

HOW IT WORKS

From PDF to exam-ready in three steps

01

Upload your materials

Drop in up to 5 PDFs per chat — 50 MB and 1,000 pages each. Scanned pages are read with Tesseract OCR automatically.

02

Ask in plain language

Scope to one book or search everything. Follow-ups like “explain it simpler” keep context, and answers stream in real time.

03

Verify and revise

Click any citation to see the exact excerpt and page. Generate ranked important topics with page references for revision.

FEATURES

Everything you need to study smarter

A familiar chat experience, built on a production-grade retrieval pipeline.

Multi-PDF ingestion & OCR

PyMuPDF extraction with page offsets, ligature cleanup and automatic OCR for scanned pages.

Semantic retrieval

200-token chunks, local 384-dim embeddings and cosine search in ChromaDB with smart deduplication.

Citation repair loop

Every claim is checked against [S1], [S2] source labels. Uncited answers are automatically repaired.

Source inspector

Clickable source chips open the verbatim excerpt, page number and the original PDF in your browser.

Important topics generator

Ranked exam topics with descriptions, importance rationale and page references. Quizzes and flashcards next.

ChatGPT-style interface

Frosted glass composer, smart auto-scroll, copy, feedback, inline rename and a stop button.

ARCHITECTURE

Production-grade, top to bottom

Async FastAPI, SQLAlchemy 2.0 with Alembic migrations, PostgreSQL job queue with leases and retries, Argon2 auth with HttpOnly sessions — and a React + TypeScript + Vite front end.
